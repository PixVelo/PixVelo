Why compress images?

Smaller image files can be easier to upload, share and store. Compression can reduce file size while balancing visual quality.

Why resize images?

Different websites and documents often need different dimensions. Resizing creates a more suitable image for the intended use.

Why convert formats?

JPG, PNG and WebP have different strengths. Conversion can help match an image to a workflow or storage goal.

Why browser-based tools?

For supported operations, browser processing means your images never need to leave your device.

Frequently asked questions

What is an image compressor?

A compressor reduces an image's file size by changing encoding settings and, depending on the tool, dimensions or quality.

How do I resize an image?

Open the Image Resizer, upload an image, enter dimensions or choose a preset, then process and download.

How can I compress an image to 100KB?

The 100KB tool searches for an approximate result. Some images cannot reach an exact target without substantial quality loss.

Does compression reduce quality?

It can. Lossy compression trades some image information for smaller files.

What formats are supported?

The tools accept JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF as inputs where supported. Output formats vary by tool.

Are these tools free?

The core tools are designed to work without an account or payment step.

Are uploaded images stored?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Images never leave your device.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive and designed for touch-friendly use.
